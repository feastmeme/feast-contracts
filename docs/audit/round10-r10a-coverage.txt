**HIGH**

**H1 — Full-balance payouts permanently strand claims exceeding the venue’s signed settlement limit.**

`TorchHook` accepts cumulative creator/referrer entitlements as `uint256`, but every withdrawal requests the entire entitlement. Its payout calls `vault.burn`, whose amount must fit a positive `int128`: at most `2^127 − 1`. This is a Torch integration defect: the ledger can grow beyond the size its withdrawal path supports. [Creator claim](src/TorchHook.sol:610), [referrer claim](src/TorchHook.sol:621), [payout](src/TorchHook.sol:589), [venue restriction](lib/infinity-core/src/libraries/SafeCast.sol:54).

A concrete source-derived construction:

1. Launch against an ordinary, non-taxing **36-decimal ERC-20**, with coin as currency0, supply `10^33`, liquidity `999 × 10^30`, opening square-root price `2^96`, starting tick zero, and zero creator allocation, self-burn share and referral share. The seed consumes approximately 99.9% of supply and satisfies the factory’s remainder check. Such quote decimals are within the plan’s stated **at-least-six** assumption. [Creation](src/TorchFactory.sol:129), [remainder check](src/TorchFactory.sol:145), [assumption](docs/plan.md:374).
2. After the two-block cap window, execute **215 exact-input buys**, each with `zeroForOne=false`, `amountSpecified=−10^38`, and price limit `2^150`, without claiming between them. Each individual input and output fits the venue’s signed range; the seeded range has ample remaining capacity.
3. Each trade banks fee `floor(10^38 / 101)`. With the selected shares, the creator receives that fee minus its floored 20% platform share. [Fee calculation](src/TorchHook.sol:279), [split](src/TorchHook.sol:927).

| Quantity | Raw quote units |
|---|---:|
| Maximum settlement amount | 170141183460469231731687303715884105727 |
| Creator pending after 214 buys | 169504950495049504950495049504950494880 |
| Creator pending after 215 buys | **170297029702970297029702970297029702800** |

The threshold is approximately **170.14 whole quote tokens** at 36 decimals.

Every subsequent creator claim reverts. `claimAsBnb` cannot rescue it: `_claimTo` attempts the same full payout before conversion. Reversion restores the oversized entitlement, future accrual only increases it, and no entry point permits a smaller withdrawal. The referrer path has the same failure. [Conversion claim settlement](src/TorchHook.sol:684), [release](src/TorchHook.sol:706).

This requires neither transfer taxation nor a partial conversion fill. Permit bounded withdrawals while preserving the unpaid remainder. The construction was checked through source inspection and integer arithmetic, **not an EVM reproduction**.
