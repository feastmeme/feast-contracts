**Completeness of the shipped mandate**

The original mandate is partly shipped through the explicit, accepted cut. I found no additional silently omitted product mechanism. V1 has **zero governed economic parameters**, and source inspection found no owner, upgrade, pause or post-creation economic setter.

| Fee share | End of the shipped path |
|---|---|
| Self-burn | Vault claims purchase the coin; the keeper receives a proportional tip and the remainder is burned. Unspent input remains attributed. The declared granularity limit applies. |
| Creator | Quote-token pull payment, or claimant-authorized conversion delivering wrapped native. Burn-address creators redirect into self-burn. |
| Referrer | Quote-token pull payment or claimant-authorized conversion. Without a valid referral, that share remains with the creator. |
| Platform | Vault-backed accumulation with no v1 spending path, as explicitly accepted. |

These destinations follow from [banking](src/TorchHook.sol:923), [claims](src/TorchHook.sol:610), and [buyback disposal](src/TorchHook.sol:774). LP quote fees remain collectable, with their platform portion redirected into self-burn; LP coin fees go to the burn address. Vesting pays its fixed recipient.

The conversion withdrawal remains atomic at every hop: refusal unwinds earlier swaps and claim retirement. Direct quote claiming remains available under ordinary token semantics. I found **no additional entitlement stranded behind that boundary**. Self-buyback accepts partial fills and debits actual spend; zero output reverts the transaction. No platform two-press transition survives in this checkout. [Per-hop enforcement](src/TorchConverter.sol:262), [buyback settlement](src/TorchHook.sol:752).
