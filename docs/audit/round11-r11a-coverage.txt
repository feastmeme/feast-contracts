**HIGH**

**H1 — A permissionless referrer claim permanently deletes the unpaid excess.**

`claimReferrer` caps `amount` at `MAX_SETTLEMENT`, then sets the entire pending entry to zero. It pays only the capped amount. Its accept set therefore includes calls that destroy someone else’s entitlement. The ordinary `claim` wrapper reaches the same path. [Cap and deletion](src/TorchHook.sol:622), [wrapper](src/TorchHook.sol:649).

Concrete construction:

1. Use the admitted 36-decimal fixture, with `referrerBps = 3000`, and supply `abi.encode(referrer)` on each buy. Keep its supply, liquidity, ordinary quote token and post-cap trading state. [Fixture](test/TorchClaimBound.t.sol:74).
2. Execute 600 buys of `10^38` raw quote units without claiming. Each referral credit is `floor(floor(10^38 / 101) × 3000 / 10000)`. [Referral banking](src/TorchHook.sol:949).
3. Any address calls `claimReferrer(key, referrer)`.

The pending balance is approximately **178.217822 quote tokens**. The call pays approximately **170.141183** and permanently erases **8.076638**, exactly `8076638321708986090094874501937676273` raw units. Its vault backing remains, but neither claim path can recover the deleted entitlement.

`claimCreator` correctly increments `creatorPaid` only by the capped payment. `_claimTo` correctly shares one settlement budget between creator and referral components and subtracts only the referral amount paid. The regression test exercises only the creator path, leaving this sibling defect undetected. [Creator debit](src/TorchHook.sol:610), [combined debit](src/TorchHook.sol:695), [witness](test/TorchClaimBound.t.sol:109).

**H2 — Accumulated LP fees can still exceed one settlement and become permanently uncollectable.**

Torch’s sole LP collection path requests the position’s entire accumulated fees with `modifyLiquidity(liquidityDelta: 0)`. There is no bounded collection amount. The venue calculates those fees as unsigned cumulative amounts, then casts each entire amount to `int128` before returning. Thus failure happens before Torch can bank or cap anything. [Torch integration](src/TorchHook.sol:551), [dependency’s narrowing](lib/infinity-core/src/pool-cl/libraries/CLPool.sol:149).

A short construction uses the same 36-decimal pool:

1. Execute one buy so the seeded position is active.
2. A funded caller makes two venue donations of `10^38` raw quote units each, settling each in a separate vault lock. Each donation individually fits the signed limit.
3. Call `collectLpFees(key)`.

The venue accepts public donations and adds them to the active position’s fee growth. Torch registers no donation callback that would collect or constrain this accumulation. [Donation entry](lib/infinity-core/src/pool-cl/CLPoolManager.sol:215), [fee growth](lib/infinity-core/src/pool-cl/libraries/CLPool.sol:463), [Torch bitmap](src/TorchCLHookBase.sol:36).

At the fixture’s liquidity, those donations alone create **`2 × 10^38 − 1` raw units** of collectable LP fees—above `MAX_SETTLEMENT`. Collection reverts; its checkpoint update rolls back. Subsequent collections encounter the same oversized entitlement, and ordinary claims cannot reach fees that were never banked.

This is another unbounded cumulative settlement in Torch’s integration. It is separate from the accepted converter stray-balance boundary.

**M2 — Two rewritten witnesses still overstate their evidence.**

**I11:** The new test does execute exact-in/out buys and sells in both orderings. That coverage gap is repaired. However, **0.1% is not an appropriate advertised rounding tolerance** for the stated invariant.

At its `1_000_000e18` input, the quote comparison permits roughly **1,000 whole quote tokens** of disagreement; the buy-fee comparison permits roughly **9.9 whole quote tokens**. The test attributes this tolerance to rounding and the corrections, while the plan promises agreement within one wei. [Eight-swap test](test/TorchFeeTake.t.sol:280), [tolerance](test/TorchFeeTake.t.sol:287), [promise](docs/plan.md:192).

Use a justified rounding bound, separating fee arithmetic from price-dependent input rounding. A 0.1% check can be described as a loose regression check; it does not establish the advertised rounding-scale equivalence.

**Real-token freeze:** The probe transfers the **placeholder token from `AAPLB_HOLDER`**, although the fixture identifies a different `placeholderHolder`. It establishes neither sufficient placeholder balance nor a successful identical transfer before pausing. [Fixture holders and funding](test/TorchEndToEnd.t.sol:299), [probe](test/TorchEndToEnd.t.sol:332).

If that account has no placeholder balance, an unfrozen token’s insufficient-balance revert satisfies `assertFalse(moved)`. The witness therefore still cannot attribute refusal to freezing. It also performs no frozen claim attempt or unpause-and-claim recovery sequence afterward. [Remaining assertions](test/TorchEndToEnd.t.sol:336).

The **young-pool witness is repaired**: it establishes positive allowance, verifies insufficient history and requires `OracleTooYoung` specifically. [Witness](test/TorchBuyback.t.sol:371).
