**HIGH — none found.**

For ordinary, non-taxing quote-token transfers, the four settlement sites satisfy the bounded-payment predicate:

| Site | Result |
|---|---|
| `claimCreator` | Pays `min(creatorPending, MAX_SETTLEMENT)` and increments `creatorPaid` by exactly that payment. [Source](src/TorchHook.sol:610) |
| `claimReferrer` | Pays `min(referrerPending, MAX_SETTLEMENT)` and subtracts exactly that payment. The unpaid remainder survives. [Source](src/TorchHook.sol:622) |
| `_claimTo` | Creator payment consumes the shared budget before referral payment is selected. Each ledger loses only its own contribution. [Source](src/TorchHook.sol:700) |
| `_release` | Opens one payout lock with the bounded amount supplied by its callers. The callback burns and transfers that same amount; transfer failure reverts the preceding ledger changes. [Primitive](src/TorchHook.sol:722), [settlement](src/TorchHook.sol:584) |

Let `M = MAX_SETTLEMENT`, with creator and referral balances `C` and `R`. `_claimTo` pays:

`a = min(C, M); b = min(R, M − a)`

Therefore `a + b ≤ M`, and the remaining entitlements are exactly `C − a` and `R − b`. A non-creator receives only the referral component. Repeated calls drain a fixed entitlement without discarding or double-counting either component.

The public `claim` wrapper can pay **two settlement-sized amounts in one transaction**, through two separate locks. This respects the per-settlement bound. No creator/referrer payout bypasses `_release`. [Wrapper](src/TorchHook.sol:654)

Other settlement operations exist outside that payout class: seed funding, buyback settlement, LP collection and direct ERC-20 vesting payment. They do not provide an alternate creator/referrer withdrawal path. [Buyback settlement](src/TorchHook.sol:529), [vesting payment](src/TorchVesting.sol:77)
