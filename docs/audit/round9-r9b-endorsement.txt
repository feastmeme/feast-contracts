**MEDIUM**

**M3 — Successful short transfers still retire full entitlements.**

Creator and referrer claims debit nominal entitlement and then burn vault claims and request the nominal transfer. There is no recipient-delivery check. An upgradeable quote introducing a 1% outbound tax can deliver 99 while permanently retiring 100; anyone can trigger that payment. [Creator debit](src/TorchHook.sol:615), [referrer debit](src/TorchHook.sol:626), [payout](src/TorchHook.sol:589).

The plan still promises balance-delta protection against this exact change in token behaviour. Enforce delivery or withdraw that guarantee. Reverting-transfer tests do not cover successful short payment. [Promise](docs/plan.md:226).

**M4 — A pending launch’s predicted address can still receive substituted economics.**

CREATE2 commits to salt, name, symbol and supply, but excludes creator, quote, shares, allocation and opening configuration. Another caller can copy the committed fields, substitute those economics, and deploy first. The intended creation then fails while its predicted address contains the substitute. [Deployment](src/TorchFactory.sol:120), [prediction](src/TorchFactory.sol:182).

The existing witness tests identical-parameter reuse only. Bind address identity to authenticated creation intent and configuration. [Witness](test/TorchFactory.t.sol:270).

**M5 — The execution checks still permit more than the advertised 3% TWAP-relative loss.**

Self-buyback permits 300 ticks of spot/TWAP displacement, then another 3% movement in **square-root spot price**. For a constant-liquidity purchase reaching the upper limit, the output factor against TWAP is approximately:

`1 / (1.03 × 1.0001^300) = 0.94218`

That is a **5.78% shortfall before fees**. The square-root limit alone permits a 6.09% terminal price increase from spot. [Spot check](src/TorchHook.sol:887), [execution limit](src/TorchHook.sol:509).

Three converter hops can compound their permitted displacement with the route shortfall to approximately `0.97 / 1.0001^900 = 0.88652`. [Route floor](src/TorchConverter.sol:184).

Specify and test the aggregate economic bound, or narrow the [maximum-slippage claim](src/TorchConstants.sol:140).

**M6 — Deployment and launch-sizing instructions remain non-executable as written.**

The recipe supplies two converter constructor arguments; three are required, including the predicted hook. [Recipe](README.md:135), [constructor](src/TorchConverter.sol:112).

The working fixture supplies that argument and predicts hook at nonce `n + 3`, factory at `n + 5`, counting from immediately before oracle deployment. Document that complete wiring. [Fixture](test/TorchEndToEnd.t.sol:102).

The liquidity approximation also omits opening price. For currency0 near square-root price 2, liquidity approximately equal to the allocation consumes roughly half the coins, triggering `PoolTooShallow`. Give the price-dependent sizing formula. [Guidance](docs/interface.md:62), [refusal](src/TorchFactory.sol:145).

**Completeness of the shipped mandate**

| Fee share | Destination |
|---|---|
| Self-burn | Vault claims fund own-pool coin purchases; the keeper receives a proportional tip and the remainder is burned. Unspent input retains entitlement. The declared granularity limit remains. |

| Referrer | Quote-token pull payment or claimant-authorized conversion. Unreferred commission remains with the creator. M3 remains. |
| Platform | Vault-backed accumulation with no v1 spending path, as explicitly accepted. |

These destinations follow from [banking](src/TorchHook.sol:923), [claims](src/TorchHook.sol:610), and [buyback disposal](src/TorchHook.sol:774).

The conversion withdrawal remains atomic at every hop: refusal unwinds earlier hops and claim retirement. Direct quote claiming remains available under ordinary token semantics. I found no additional remainder stranded behind that boundary and no surviving platform two-press transition to leave inconsistent. [Per-hop enforcement](src/TorchConverter.sol:262).
