**M2 — The internal fill requires coins physically present before the seller settles, violating swap admission.**

The hook immediately takes and burns coins during `beforeSwap`. The venue’s `take` performs an actual token transfer; matching transient deltas do not guarantee sufficient physical balance at that instant. [Fill settlement](src/FeastHook.sol:480), [venue transfer](lib/infinity-core/src/Vault.sol:143).

A constructable witness uses an open pool with a positive fill allowance:

1. Within one vault lock, a router temporarily takes the vault’s coin balance.
2. It submits an exact-input sale, intending to return the borrowed coins and settle its sale before releasing the lock.
3. Without the fill, the pool can account for the sale and accept settlement afterward.
4. With any positive fill, the hook’s immediate coin transfer fails because the vault is temporarily empty.

Thus an otherwise settleable operation reverts because of the optional fill. The existing router fixture also settles inputs only after `swap` returns. [Settlement ordering](test/utils/PoolRouter.sol:61).

Bound the immediate fill by available coin custody, or skip it when custody is insufficient. Perform that decision before debiting claims or buckets, and recompute quote payment after reducing the fill. Catching only the failed transfer after earlier effects would introduce an accounting violation.

The buyback executes a venue swap, observes its resulting price and emits `BoughtBackAndBurned`, but never invokes `_emitTrade`. Its callbacks are skipped because the hook is itself the swapper. Consequently, a chart consuming the promised single event stream misses buyback volume and price changes. [Buyback swap](src/FeastHook.sol:742), [return after observation](src/FeastHook.sol:766), [terminal event](src/FeastHook.sol:1074).

Emit one `Trade` for each successful buyback using its venue delta, post-swap price and zero Feast fee. Use coin output before tip/burn distribution. This preserves the gate and avoids adding ledger credit or duplicate events.
