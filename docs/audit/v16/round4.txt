**HIGH**

**H1 — Moving the platform price after the grace period disables the value cap.**

`Moved` prevents settlement only while `!graceOver`. Afterwards it enters the token-cap-only branch, although the platform pool already has mature price history. [FeastEmissions.sol:263](src/FeastEmissions.sol:263)

A trader with credits in an unsettled, closed week can wait three days, move FEAST’s spot more than 300 ticks from its average, settle, and reverse the price move in the same transaction. The manipulation’s duration need not change the TWAP. With all tracks populated, week 10 can distribute approximately **4.43 million FEAST**, even where 1 BNB of fees and a price of 1,000 FEAST/BNB should limit distribution to **150 FEAST**.

This is a concrete post-launch bypass of the two-cap mechanism, independent of the accepted pre-TGE rule. The existing value-cap test explicitly requires `Status.Ok`, so it misses this path. [FeastEmissions.t.sol:188](test/FeastEmissions.t.sol:188)

Fix: retain the refusal for `Moved` after grace; preserve the separately authorized pre-launch fallback. This introduces no new accounting violation, though settlement must wait for an acceptable platform price.

**MEDIUM**

**M1 — Empty tracks let the redirect exceed its 50% bound.**

The redirect is calculated from `cap` before empty tracks are skipped. Consequently, the escrow receives a larger fraction of what is actually emitted than the configured percentage. [FeastEmissions.sol:276](src/FeastEmissions.sol:276)

Construct an early week containing only unreferred platform-coin trades: the trader track exists, while the creator and referrer tracks are empty. With `cap = 10,000` and redirect `5000`:

- Escrow receives 5,000.
- Traders receive 2,250.
- Actual emission is 7,250; **68.97% goes to escrow**.

The existing redirect test populates all three tracks and therefore cannot expose this case. [FeastEmissions.t.sol:299](test/FeastEmissions.t.sol:299)

Fix: calculate the nonempty tracks’ emission first, then redirect a bounded fraction of those amounts. Preserve empty-track non-emission and rounding conservation; that fix introduces no new violation.

**M2 — Genesis attempts to deploy an oversized script contract.**

Inside the broadcast region, `Genesis.launch` executes `new DeployPackage2()` solely to call `platformPoolKey`. [Genesis.s.sol:56](script/Genesis.s.sol:56), [Genesis.s.sol:63](script/Genesis.s.sol:63)

An independent flattened compilation using Solidity 0.8.26, Cancun, via-IR and 20,000 optimizer runs produced **42,489 runtime bytes** and the compiler’s 24,576-byte-limit warning. Once placeholders are populated, this deployment cannot succeed under that limit, so the script cannot reach window deployment or genesis.

Fix: share the pool-key calculation through an internal library/helper. Removing this deployment does not disturb the platform token’s prediction or genesis-bound recipients. Validate the actual broadcast transaction sequence with normal code-size limits.

**LOW**

**L1 — The fill cutoff silently disables layer 2 for plausible six-decimal markets.**

The “every real market” assertion treats raw-unit ratios as though decimals were irrelevant. [FeastHook.sol:499](src/FeastHook.sol:499)

For an 18-decimal coin worth $0.000001 and a six-decimal quote worth $100:

`raw quote/coin = 10^-20`

When the coin sorts first, `sqrtPriceX96 ≈ 7,922,816,251,426,433,759`, below `2^64`. The inverse ordering also falls outside the permitted domain. These markets trade normally but never receive an internal fill, regardless of allowance or oracle maturity. Six-decimal quotes are within the stated assumption. [docs/plan.md:374](docs/plan.md:374)

The current precision witness tests a price near 2.25, not this boundary. [FeastInternalFill.t.sol:355](test/FeastInternalFill.t.sol:355)

Fix: support the required raw-price range with exact, bounded arithmetic. **Simply removing the guard introduces overflow**, and restoring successive floors reintroduces the previous rounding defect.

**L2 — Two-hop price overflow escapes the unpriceable policy and blocks settlement.**

Each hop can produce a representable ratio while their composition overflows. There is no guard around that multiplication or catch at the emissions call site. [FeastPricer.sol:130](src/FeastPricer.sol:130), [FeastEmissions.sol:239](src/FeastEmissions.sol:239)

For two forward hops with `sqrtPriceX96 = 2^136`, each ratio is `2^208` in Q128. Composition requires `2^288`, exceeding `uint256`. Both square-root prices lie within the venue’s price range; sufficient history and matching spot/average ticks do not prevent this overflow.

The revert blocks the whole week—even if that quote earned no fees—and still blocks it after three days. Sequential settlement also blocks later weeks. The existing “unpriceable, not reverted” test covers insufficient history, not arithmetic failure. [FeastPackageTwo.t.sol:49](test/FeastPackageTwo.t.sol:49)

Fix: classify unrepresentable route prices as `Unpriceable` and preserve the grace-then-zero behavior at the consumer boundary. This introduces no new violation if valid prices retain their existing calculation.
