**HIGH**

**H1 — A bounded referrer payout permanently erases the unpaid remainder.**

`claimReferrer` computes `min(pending, MAX_SETTLEMENT)`, then sets the **entire** pending entry to zero before releasing that bounded amount. For an entitlement exceeding the limit, the call succeeds while deleting entitlement it never paid. Both `claimReferrer` and its `claim` wrapper are permissionless, so a stranger can trigger the loss. [Bound, deletion and payout](src/TorchHook.sol:625), [wrapper](src/TorchHook.sol:649)

A source-derived construction uses the existing [36-decimal fixture](test/TorchClaimBound.t.sol:55), changes `referrerBps` to `3000`, and supplies the same valid referral on every buy. Each `10^38` exact-input buy banks referral commission:

`floor(floor(10^38 / 101) × 3000 / 10000)`.

The referral entitlement exceeds `MAX_SETTLEMENT` after **573 buys**. After 1,000 buys:

| Quantity | Whole quote tokens, approximately |
|---|---:|
| Referral entitlement | 297.029703 |
| Payment | 170.141183 |
| Entitlement erased | **126.888520** |

The unpaid backing remains in the hook’s vault claims, but no pending ledger owns it. Future commissions start from zero; neither another claim nor `claimAsBnb` can recover the erased balance. The relevant accrual is [per-referrer banking](src/TorchHook.sol:945).

The new regression test exercises **creator claiming only**, with referral share zero. Its mutation likewise removes only the creator bound, so neither catches this sibling defect. [Fixture configuration](test/TorchClaimBound.t.sol:81), [assertions](test/TorchClaimBound.t.sol:119), [mutation](tools/mutate.py:44)

Subtract the payment from `_referrerPending` instead of clearing it. Add boundary/remainder witnesses for direct referral claims and combined creator/referrer conversion claims.

The other inspected payout arithmetic is correct:

- `claimCreator` increments paid by the capped amount. [Debit](src/TorchHook.sol:614)
- `_claimTo` shares one settlement budget across both components and subtracts only what each contributes. [Combined budget](src/TorchHook.sol:695)
- `claim` can make two separate bounded settlements; their combined transaction total need not fit one delta.
- No additional cumulative-entitlement path bypassing these bounds was found. Fee accrual, buyback and LP settlement amounts originate from checked or venue-returned signed deltas.

**M2 — Two rewritten witnesses still do not establish their advertised guarantees.**

**The real-token freeze premise remains unproven.** The test obtains placeholder tokens from `placeholderHolder`, but its operational probe impersonates **`AAPLB_HOLDER`** when transferring the different placeholder token. It never establishes that this account holds the placeholder or can make the same transfer before freezing. Insufficient balance or address screening can therefore produce the passing `moved == false` result without a freeze. [Funding](test/TorchEndToEnd.t.sol:305), [probe](test/TorchEndToEnd.t.sol:332)

Use a funded account and establish that the same transfer succeeds unfrozen, fails frozen, then succeeds after unfreezing. The current witness also performs no frozen claim attempt or subsequent claim recovery; its unchanged-accrual read cannot establish those behaviours.

**I11 now covers eight real swaps, but 0.1% is not the right tolerance to advertise as rounding-scale equivalence.** The snapshots and buy/sell comparisons genuinely execute both orderings. However, the helper labels 0.1% as venue rounding plus rate corrections without deriving that bound. On its one-million-token input, the quote-payment assertion permits approximately **1,000 tokens** of disagreement. That does not support the plan’s “within rounding” or one-wei statements. [Eight-swap witness and tolerance](test/TorchFeeTake.t.sol:280), [I11](docs/plan.md:396), [one-wei promise](docs/plan.md:192)

Use a derived integer-rounding bound, including any propagation through the other currency, and apply the per-branch fee identity in both orderings. The existing [two-unit branch assertion](test/TorchFeeTake.t.sol:148) is materially stronger than the new relative comparison. No underlying fee-pricing exploit is established by this evidence finding.

The **young-pool witness does hold its claim**: it establishes positive allowance, establishes insufficient history and checks `OracleTooYoung` specifically. Removing age protection would no longer leave it green through `NothingToBuyBackWith`. [Repaired witness](test/TorchBuyback.t.sol:371)
