**MEDIUM**

**M1 — LP fee redirection bypasses the accrual checkpoint.**

For a non-burn creator with `selfBurnBps == 0`, `_bank` skips `_checkpointAllowance` because its local `selfBurn` is zero. It subsequently adds the LP fees’ platform share to `selfBurnEarned`. Thus pending grows without settling accrual. [Conditional checkpoint](src/TorchHook.sol:941), [later increase](src/TorchHook.sol:950)

Two constructions follow:

- The first LP collection can create a 1,000-token burn bucket while `allowanceUpdatedAt` remains zero. It immediately exposes 50 tokens, although the bucket has existed for zero seconds.
- After exhausting allowance, leave 1,000 tokens pending for 3,599 seconds, then bank another 9,000 through LP redirection. Available allowance becomes 500 tokens instead of the checkpointed 50.

This violates both empty-start and no-retroactive-credit promises. The regression test banks ordinary swap fees with a positive self-burn share, so it misses this route. [Test](test/TorchBuyback.t.sol:670)

Compute the complete burn credit, including redirected LP income, before deciding whether to checkpoint. Add first-collection and interleaved-collection witnesses.

**M2 — The allowance still strands otherwise executable buckets.**

The cap depends on raw quote quantity, without accounting for the quote required to buy one raw coin unit. Waiting never increases an ordinary bucket’s allowance beyond 5%; zero-output purchases revert. [Cap](src/TorchHook.sol:810), [refusal](src/TorchHook.sol:757)

A source-derived integer construction uses a six-decimal quote, coin as currency0, supply 1,000 raw coin units, opening tick `138200`, and liquidity `1001901`. The seed consumes 1,000 units. After opening caps expire, buying 300 coins exact-output and selling 300 back produces approximately 1,718,885 raw quote units of self-burn entitlement.

Its 85,944-unit maximum allowance buys **zero** raw coins. Spending the whole bucket would buy **one**, moving square-root price only approximately 0.171%, inside the execution limit. Collecting the outstanding LP fees does not close that gap. The factory admits this granularity. [Creation and seeding](src/TorchFactory.sol:120)

With trading stopped, every permitted press fails indefinitely. Address executable output granularity; quote decimals alone do not establish spendability.

**M3 — Direct claims retire nominal entitlement without verifying delivery.**

Creator and referrer claims debit the full entitlement, then burn vault claims and request the nominal transfer. They never verify the recipient’s balance increase. An upgradeable quote that starts taxing outbound transfers can deliver 99 while retiring 100; anyone can trigger that settlement. [Creator debit](src/TorchHook.sol:610), [referrer debit](src/TorchHook.sol:621), [payout](src/TorchHook.sol:589)

This contradicts the explicit protection against quotes becoming fee-on-transfer. [Promise](docs/plan.md:226)

Enforce the promised delivery condition or explicitly withdraw it. Tests of transfers that revert do not cover successful short delivery.

**M4 — CREATE2 permits substitution of a pending launch’s economics.**

The address commits to salt, name, symbol and supply, but excludes creator, quote, shares, allocation and opening configuration. Another caller can copy the committed fields, substitute those economics, and deploy first. The intended transaction then fails while its predicted address contains the substituted launch. [Deployment](src/TorchFactory.sol:120), [prediction](src/TorchFactory.sol:182)

The existing test checks identical-parameter reuse, not competing creation intent. [Test](test/TorchFactory.t.sol:270)

Bind deployment identity to authenticated creation intent and configuration. This remains acknowledged as open in the [census](docs/site-census.md:257).

**M5 — Execution permits greater TWAP-relative loss than the advertised 3% bound.**

Self-buyback permits a 300-tick spot/TWAP displacement, then multiplies **square-root spot price** by 0.97 or 1.03. The upper limit permits terminal price to rise 6.09% from that already displaced spot. [Execution limit](src/TorchHook.sol:509), [spot check](src/TorchHook.sol:873)

A constant-liquidity purchase reaching the upper limit can receive approximately:

`1 / (1.03 × 1.0001^300) = 0.94218`

of TWAP-priced output before fees—a **5.78% shortfall**.

Three converter hops can compound their allowed displacement with the route shortfall to approximately `0.97 / 1.0001^900 = 0.88652`. [Route floor](src/TorchConverter.sol:184)

Specify and test the aggregate economic bound, or narrow the [maximum-slippage claim](src/TorchConstants.sol:132).

**M6 — Deployment and launch-sizing instructions are not executable generally as written.**

**Completeness of the shipped mandate**

The platform/rewards/governance cut is explicit and accepted. There are **zero governed economic parameters in v1**.

| Fee share | Destination and conclusion |
|---|---|
| Self-burn | Vault claims fund coin purchases; the keeper receives a proportional tip and the rest is burned. Unspent input retains entitlement. M2 prevents a universal spendability claim. |
| Creator | Quote-token pull payment or claimant-authorized conversion to **wrapped native**. Burn-address creators redirect into self-burn. Short delivery remains M3. |
| Referrer | Quote-token pull payment or claimant-authorized conversion. Unreferred commission remains with the creator. |
| Platform | Vault-backed accumulation with no v1 spending path, as explicitly accepted. This review establishes its present custody/accounting, not future cross-version spending capability. |

These destinations follow from [banking](src/TorchHook.sol:913), [claims](src/TorchHook.sol:610), and [buyback disposal](src/TorchHook.sol:773). LP quote income is collected, with its platform portion redirected to self-burn; LP coin income goes to the burn address. Vesting pays its fixed recipient. [LP settlement](src/TorchHook.sol:569), [vesting](src/TorchVesting.sol:77)

The converter’s full-consumption refusal is atomic at every hop. Failure unwinds earlier hops and preserves the quote claim; direct claiming remains available under ordinary token semantics. I found no additional remainder stranded behind that withdrawal. No platform two-press transition survives in this tree. [Per-hop enforcement](src/TorchConverter.sol:262)

On self-buyback, cap and refill use the same pending base within each calculation. Rounding floors accrual; repeated checkpoints do not manufacture fractional credit. I found no untrusted callback between allowance calculation and debit that can profitably change this pool’s pending. The LP checkpoint omission and dust exception remain as described above.

**I would not endorse this internal v1 until the remaining accounting, spendability, documentation and evidence defects are resolved.**
