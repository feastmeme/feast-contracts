## Owner to confirm at the genesis-parameter review

Two constants are new in v1.6.24 and are the owner's to adjust before the ceremony. Each is one
constant in `src/FeastGenesis.sol`, and `M2CoinAsCurrency0::test_theTwoConstantsTheOwnerConfirms`
states both values, so a change shows there and nowhere else:

| constant | value | what it decides |
|---|---|---|
| `MIN_POT` | `1 ether` | the smallest launch-window pot that counts as participation; below it nothing is sold and every commit is refunded |
| `ABANDON_AFTER` | `7 days` | how long after the window closes its auction may still clear (plus every frozen second); after it, the pool opens with nothing sold and refunds open |

## The fixes

**M-1.** `claimAsBnb(key, account, route, minOut)` forwards the beneficiary's bound to
`convert(tokenIn, amountIn, route, recipient, minOut)`, which reverts `BelowMinOut(got, minOut)`
**after** its 3% route floor. Zero keeps exactly the old protection: the floor is kept and checked
first. It still refuses a route whose pools each sit inside their bands but compose badly; it does
not defend the price a claimant saw before sending, and the converter's docstrings now say so.
Each measured attack — one hop at the band edge (9,704 bps), band × floor (9,413), three hops
(8,865) — is kept and is refused by a 99.5% bound. Each also records what zero still pays, and
the bound is pinned at exactly the realised amount. Two paths do not consult `minOut`: a pool
quoted in wrapped native, which converts nothing (as `route` is already ignored), and a claimant
owed nothing, who gets 0. Holding those to `minOut` measured +83 B the hook does not have. Not
done: anchoring the floor to the averages instead of spot. That would change every route's
admission arithmetic, and the beneficiary's bound already caps the worst case.

**M-2** (shape ruled by the coordinator on 2026-10-01).
- A pot below `MIN_POT`, or one whose clearing price cannot be represented in the venue's range,
  is **no participation**. `settle` opens the pool single-sided at the genesis opening, sells
  nothing, and every commit is refundable, in both currency orderings.
  `ClearingPriceUnrepresentable` and `ClearingPriceOutOfRange` are gone: no input makes `settle`
  revert for arithmetic, and no dust buys the tranche.
- `settle` has **no deadline**: the coin can always reach the pool. The **auction** expires at
  `abandonAt() = endsAt + ABANDON_AFTER + guardian.frozenSeconds()`. From then on, `settle` takes
  the no-participation branch and an unsettled window refunds.
- `refund` lowers `total`, as `withdraw` always did, so `total` is always the live pot. That
  closes the `RefundedWeightRevival` class a second way.
- `settle` refuses under an ALL freeze, as its first statement (`_requireUnfrozen`). `commit`,
  `withdraw`, `claim` and `refund` stay open: they move the committers' own money, and
  `withdraw`'s deadline must not be eaten by a freeze.
- `frozenSeconds()` grows no faster than the clock. So the brake can delay `settle` but cannot
  push an auction into abandonment, and refunds that have opened cannot close.

Every PoC scenario is kept in its ordering and asserts the new outcome. In the currency1
ordering: the one-wei commit, the 16,427,305 wei threshold, the year-later strand, and the freeze
across the hour. In the currency0 ordering: one wei buying 30% of the supply, and settle after the
hour. New tests cover the floor and `abandonAt` from both sides, the freeze's exact extension, and
the live pot.

**L-2.** `consult` clamps the window **up** to `MIN_OBSERVATION_AGE` and reverts
`OracleTooYoung(age, window)` unless the history covers it; the error is unchanged, so no ABI
moves. Every caller handles the refusal:
- the internal fill asks with `try` and fills nothing;
- `buyBackAndBurn` refuses the press;
- `FeastPricer.platformPerNativeQ128` guarded on `MIN_OBSERVATION_AGE`, the oracle's old refusal
  point. Left there, it would have reverted `OracleTooYoung(400, 600)` in the pool's sixth to
  tenth minutes, measured, and `FeastEmissions.settle` with it. It guards on the whole
  `TWAP_WINDOW` now.

Once a pool is older than the window, the history never runs short: the oracle writes at most one
observation per second into 1,024 slots. **Declared boundary:** `FeastConverter._averageTick` and
`FeastPricer._vouch` still shorten the window to a PancakeSwap v3 pool's own history, floored at
300 s. That history belongs to a third-party pool whose cardinality feast does not set, and
requiring 600 s there could refuse live routes. For the converter, M-1's `minOut` bounds the
claimant whatever the window.

**L-1 — accepted, declared boundary.** The opening purchase cap is per wallet, for a pool's first
`CAP_BLOCKS` (2) served blocks, by design (`FeastHook.sol:1048`). It bounds any one wallet, not a
crowd of wallets. Each extra wallet pays its own way: its own gas, and the 1% fee on every buy.

**I-1 — accepted and documented.** Binding keys on `tx.origin`. A smart-account user whose UserOp
a bundler relays gets no attribution, and a bundler whose own address is bound has its referrer
paid on every unbound trade it relays. This is a known boundary of lifetime binding: no on-chain
rule can recognise the account behind a UserOp without trusting the bundler.
`docs/interface.md`'s referral section says so for integrators.

**I-2, I-3, I-10 — deferred.** I-2 (rounding direction of the average tick) is informational, to
be unified in a later version with a test, not five days before mainnet. I-3 is gas and
readability only. I-10: the comments carry the audit history on purpose, to be trimmed after
mainnet.

**I-4 — accepted, by design.** Creating a coin before genesis is the product's calendar, not a
gap. Package 1 opens the factory and the platform coin's TGE comes when the owner decides (Q25;
timeline A opens the factory first). Emission week 1 is the week the factory opens, and pre-launch
weeks have a written rule. Every pre-genesis state is handled:
- the platform-fee exit refuses `PlatformSinkNotBound` and the share stays owned;
- the guardian's brake is live before genesis.

A `require(genesisDone)` in `create` would close the factory until the TGE.

**I-5.** A pre-buy must vest for at least `MIN_PREBUY_VESTING` = 7 days, refused by name in
`create`, beside the other pre-buy refusals and before anything moves. Seven days is the launch
form's default and its shortest non-zero option (`feast-app` `src/lib/chain/prebuy.ts`:
`[0, 7, 30, 90, 180, 365]`, default 7). A launch without a pre-buy reads no duration. **App
consequence:** drop the 0-day option and its "sellable at once" copy.

**I-6.** Both usable-tick helpers use `TickMath.MIN_TICK`/`MAX_TICK` from the MIT-licensed
`uniswap-v4-core` library, which the factory and window already import. These are constants, so
no test can fail on the base. The witness is the bytecode, which does not move.

**I-7.** One re-entrancy guard is shared by `claim`, `claimAsBnb`, `buyBackAndBurn`,
`burnFilledCoin` and `collectLpFees`. It is never on `lockAcquired` or the venue callbacks, which
the venue calls from inside those functions and which are authenticated by caller. The guard is a
transient flag at slot 0 of the hook's own transient storage. The error keeps OpenZeppelin's name,
`ReentrancyGuardReentrantCall()`. OpenZeppelin's own contracts were measured and do not fit:
`ReentrancyGuard` +322 B, `ReentrancyGuardTransient` +317 B, against 107 B of margin. This guard
costs +97 B. All five were already checks-effects-interactions, so this is depth.
`claimCreator`, `claimReferrer` and `releasePlatformFees` have the same CEI shape and are not
guarded, for bytes.

**I-8.** Both transfers to `0x…dEaD` are real burns now:
- the seed's unplaced remainder (`burnUnusedSeed`, reached from both factory seeding paths);
- the coin side of the locked position's fees. `collectLpFees` mints it as a claim owed to
  `burnFilledCoin`, which already burns the fill's coin. +28 B; a burn inside the lock measured
  +95 B.

There is no case where the coin is not burnable: every coin either path disposes of is a
`FeastToken` or the platform coin, both `ERC20Burnable`.

**I-9.** A window deployed after its `startsAt` opens at its deploying block, and `endsAt` and
`lockAt` follow from the clamped start. Genesis reads `endsAt` from the window, so the team's
vesting start and the sink's opening follow it.
