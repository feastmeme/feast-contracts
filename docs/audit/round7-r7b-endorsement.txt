**HIGH**

**H1 — The allowance does not preserve the promised 20%-per-hour spending ceiling.**

The implementation limits available allowance at an instant. It does not limit cumulative spending within an hour. A previously unpressed pool starts full, then refills immediately after spending. [Initial allowance and refill](src/TorchHook.sol:797)

For a mature, sufficiently deep pool, with no intervening fee accrual:

| Time | Pending before press | Permitted spend |
|---|---:|---:|
| Start | 1,000 | 200 |
| 30 minutes later | 800 | 80 |

That is **280 units—28% of the original bucket—in half an hour**. Waiting almost an hour before the second press approaches 36%. Both requests obey the implementation. This directly contradicts the explicit replacement intent and README promise. [Intent](docs/plan.md:748), [promise](README.md:66)

There is a second acceleration within this mechanism: newly banked principal receives retroactive allowance. `_bank` changes `selfBurnEarned` without checkpointing allowance; the next calculation applies the **new** cap to all elapsed time since the previous press. [Banking](src/TorchHook.sol:914), [calculation](src/TorchHook.sol:806)

For example, after spending the initial 200 above, leave pending at 800 for 1,799 seconds, then bank 9,200 more units. At second 1,800 the calculation grants 1,000 units, although integrating the stated rate over the principal’s actual lifetime grants approximately 80.511.

The current refill test explicitly accepts the first behaviour but never measures cumulative outflow; it contains no intervening accrual. [Test](test/TorchBuyback.t.sol:425)

Define and enforce the intended spending envelope, including initial burst credit and principal changes. Checkpoint accrual when its principal changes, and add sequence properties measuring realised outflow across sliding windows. Alternatively, the broader spending envelope needs an explicit owner ruling and corresponding documentation; it is not the ratified “unchanged” ceiling.

**MEDIUM**

**M1 — The allowance still permanently blocks some otherwise executable buckets.**

The exemption depends only on raw quote quantity. Above 10,000 units, allowance never exceeds 20% of pending, regardless of how much quote buys one raw coin unit. [Cap](src/TorchHook.sol:797)

A factory-admissible coin-as-currency0 configuration can have supply 1,000 raw coin units, opening tick `138200`, and liquidity `1,001,901`; the position consumes 1,000 units. The factory imposes no minimum supply or price/granularity relationship. [Creation](src/TorchFactory.sol:120), [seed validation](src/TorchFactory.sol:145)

Around that price, with a six-decimal quote:

- Pending of approximately 2,000,000 quote units gives an allowance of approximately 400,000.
- Spending 400,000 produces zero raw coin units.
- Spending the whole 2,000,000 produces one raw coin unit, with approximately 0.2% square-root-price movement—inside the existing limit.

With trading stopped and LP fees collected, every permitted press reverts `BoughtNothing`; waiting never increases allowance beyond its cap. The bucket is executable in aggregate but inaccessible through this button. [Refusal](src/TorchHook.sol:757)

Address executable output granularity, rather than assuming quote decimals alone establish spendability. The new shallow-pool test does not close this case.

**M2 — Direct claims retire entitlement without verifying delivery.**

Creator/referrer bookkeeping retires the nominal amount, then burns vault claims and requests the corresponding transfer. No recipient balance measurement verifies delivery. A quote token that starts taxing outgoing transfers can deliver 99 while permanently settling entitlement of 100. Anyone can trigger that settlement. [Creator debit](src/TorchHook.sol:610), [referrer debit](src/TorchHook.sol:621), [payout](src/TorchHook.sol:589)

This contradicts the explicit balance-delta protection promised for quotes becoming fee-on-transfer. [Promise](docs/plan.md:226)

Enforce exact delivery or explicitly withdraw that guarantee. Pause/revert tests do not exercise successful short delivery.

**M3 — CREATE2 still permits substitution of a pending launch’s economics.**

The address commits to salt, name, symbol and supply, but excludes creator, quote, shares, allocation, vesting duration and opening configuration. Another caller can copy the committed fields, substitute those economics, and deploy first. The intended transaction then fails while its predicted address identifies a different launch. [Deployment](src/TorchFactory.sol:120), [prediction](src/TorchFactory.sol:182)

The test checks identical-parameter reuse, not competing intent. [Test](test/TorchFactory.t.sol:270)

Bind deployment identity to authenticated creation intent and configuration. This remains explicitly recorded as open. [Census admission](docs/site-census.md:254)

**M4 — Execution permits greater TWAP-relative loss than the advertised 3% bound.**

Self-buyback allows a 300-tick spot/TWAP displacement and then multiplies **square-root price** by 0.97 or 1.03. The latter permits terminal price to rise 6.09% relative to the already displaced spot. [Execution limit](src/TorchHook.sol:509), [spot check](src/TorchHook.sol:851)

For a constant-liquidity purchase reaching the upper limit, those tolerances combine to deliver approximately:

`1 / (1.03 × 1.0001^300) = 0.94218`

of TWAP-priced output before fees: a 5.78% shortfall.

Three converter hops can similarly combine their permitted displacements with the additional route shortfall:

`0.97 / 1.0001^900 = 0.88652`

relative to corresponding TWAP pricing, before pool fees. [Route expectation](src/TorchConverter.sol:150), [output floor](src/TorchConverter.sol:184)

These do not establish the stated maximum move from the time-weighted price. [Claim](src/TorchConstants.sol:120) Specify and test the aggregate economic bound, or narrow the claims.

**Completeness of the shipped mandate**

The full product is deliberately reduced. The accepted platform/rewards/governance cut is explicit and is not a finding. There are **zero governed economic parameters in v1**.

| Fee share | Destination in v1 |
|---|---|
| Self-burn | Vault claims fund coin purchases; the keeper receives a proportional tip and the remainder is burned. Unfilled input retains entitlement. H1 and M1 prevent endorsement of its pacing/liveness guarantees. |
| Creator | Quote-token pull claim or claimant-authorized conversion to **wrapped native**. Burn-address creators redirect into self-burn. Short delivery remains M2. |
| Referrer | Quote-token pull claim or claimant-authorized conversion. Unreferred commissions remain with the creator. |
| Platform | Vault-backed accumulation with no v1 spending path, as expressly accepted. This review establishes custody/accounting, not a future cross-version spending capability. |

The destinations are implemented in [banking](src/TorchHook.sol:891), [claims](src/TorchHook.sol:610), and [buyback disposal](src/TorchHook.sol:773).

LP quote income is collected, with its platform portion redirected to self-burn. LP coin income goes to the burn address. Vesting pays the fixed recipient. [LP settlement](src/TorchHook.sol:569), [LP redirection](src/TorchHook.sol:921), [vesting payment](src/TorchVesting.sol:77)

The converter’s per-hop full-consumption boundary remains atomic and independent of unsolicited balances. I found no new remainder stranded by that refusal under ordinary token semantics: failure preserves the quote claim, and direct claiming remains available. No two-platform-press transition survives in this tree. [Boundary enforcement](src/TorchConverter.sol:262)

Within self-buyback, accrual rounding floors credit rather than increasing it; settlement uses the venue’s signed delta, and repeated dust presses create no flat-tip entitlement. I found no untrusted callback between allowance calculation and debit on that path that changes pending. These conclusions do not repair H1’s spending envelope.

Documented live entry-point signatures match the source and checked-in ABIs. The constructor and behavioural discrepancies above remain. **I would not endorse this internal v1 until the spending, spendability, delivery, documentation and evidence defects are resolved.**
