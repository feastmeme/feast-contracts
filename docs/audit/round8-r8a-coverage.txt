**MEDIUM**

**M1 — LP fee redirection still grants retroactive allowance. Surviving round-7 defect.**

`_bank` checkpoints only when its local `selfBurn` amount is nonzero. Afterwards, LP collection independently adds the platform share to `selfBurnEarned`. With an ordinary creator and `selfBurnBps = 0`, that second credit changes pending without checkpointing. Both configuration and collection are admitted. [Share calculation](src/TorchHook.sol:913), [conditional checkpoint](src/TorchHook.sol:941), [unchecked credit](src/TorchHook.sol:950).

Concrete construction: create such a pool, accumulate LP quote fees through ordinary trading, and let its oracle mature. Its self-burn bucket and allowance timestamp remain zero. Anyone collects 5,000 quote tokens of LP income, banking 1,000 into self-burn. Reading allowance immediately returns **50 tokens**, because elapsed time is measured from timestamp zero. The promised empty start would return zero. [Collection path](src/TorchHook.sol:867), [elapsed-time calculation](src/TorchHook.sol:822).

The defect also survives later: after spending those 50, leave pending at 950 for 3,599 seconds, then collect enough LP income to add another 10,000. Allowance jumps from **47.5 to 547.5** immediately. Correct checkpointing would retain 47.5 at that instant.

This prematurely exposes newly banked protocol funds to public execution. The corrected cap still limits the resulting non-dust burst to 5%, so this residual defect is MEDIUM. Compute the complete self-burn credit, including redirected LP income, before deciding whether to checkpoint.

**M2 — Output granularity still makes otherwise executable buckets permanently unspendable. Known open.**

The maximum allowance depends exclusively on raw quote quantity. Above 10,000 units, waiting never raises it beyond 5% of pending; buying zero coin reverts. [Cap](src/TorchHook.sol:810), [zero-output refusal](src/TorchHook.sol:757).

An admitted construction uses a six-decimal quote, coin as currency0, supply **1,000 raw coin units**, opening tick **138200**, liquidity **1,001,901**, and no creator allocation. After the cap window, buy 300 raw coins exact-output and sell them back. This accrues approximately 1.719 million raw quote units to self-burn. Even crediting all available LP quote income leaves the maximum allowance below approximately **86,805 units**. [Launch acceptance](src/TorchFactory.sol:105), [seed acceptance](src/TorchFactory.sol:145).

The source-derived arithmetic model returns **zero raw coins at that allowance**, but **one at the whole bucket**, with approximately 0.17% square-root-price movement. Once trading stops and the oracle matures, every permitted press fails indefinitely.

This remains a refusal that strands an executable entitlement. B4’s statement that “stranding disappears” is unsupported. [Claim](docs/plan.md:756).

**M3 — Successful short transfers permanently retire full claims. Known open.**

Anyone can claim a creator’s nominal 100-token entitlement after an upgradeable quote introduces a 1% outbound tax. The recipient receives 99, while bookkeeping retires all 100 and the vault burns all corresponding claims. Referrer claims have the same gap. No recipient-delivery measurement rejects the short payment. [Creator debit](src/TorchHook.sol:610), [referrer debit](src/TorchHook.sol:621), [payout](src/TorchHook.sol:589).

This contradicts the explicitly promised protection against quotes becoming fee-on-transfer. Require exact delivery or withdraw that guarantee. [Promise](docs/plan.md:226).

**M4 — CREATE2 permits substitution of a pending launch’s economics. Known open.**

Another caller can copy salt, name, symbol and supply, substitute creator, quote, shares, allocation and opening configuration, and deploy first. Those substituted fields are absent from the address commitment. Single-sided creation requires no contributed quote. The intended transaction then fails while its predicted address contains different economics. [Deployment](src/TorchFactory.sol:120), [address commitment](src/TorchFactory.sol:182).

Bind deployment identity to authenticated creation intent and its economic configuration.

**M5 — Execution admits losses beyond the advertised TWAP-relative band. Known open.**

A mature self-buyback pool may have spot displaced adversely by 300 ticks. The purchase then permits an additional 3% movement in **square-root spot price**. With sufficient accrued allowance, a constant-liquidity currency0 purchase reaching that limit delivers approximately:

`1 / (1.03 × 1.0001^300) = 0.94218`

of TWAP-priced output before fees: a **5.78% shortfall**. An ordering adversary can supply the initial displacement and back-run the purchase. [Spot check](src/TorchHook.sol:873), [execution limit](src/TorchHook.sol:509).

Three converter hops can compound permitted adverse spot displacements with the route’s additional tolerance to approximately `0.97 / 1.0001^900 = 0.88652` of corresponding TWAP-priced output before pool fees. [Route floor](src/TorchConverter.sol:184), [hop checks](src/TorchConverter.sol:226).

These checks do not establish R8’s stated economic bound. [R8](docs/plan.md:427).

**L2 — Frequent banking discards fractional accrual and can suppress allowance. Fresh.**

Each checkpoint floors accrued allowance and resets its timestamp, retaining no fractional remainder. Start with pending 10,001 and zero allowance, then bank one raw self-burn unit every second. For 3,599 seconds, each checkpoint calculates less than one unit and rounds it to zero. Pending reaches **13,600 while allowance remains zero**; uninterrupted accrual against the original bucket would have filled its 500-unit cap. [Checkpoint](src/TorchHook.sol:802), [rounding](src/TorchHook.sol:829).

Small ordinary swaps can produce those one-unit credits. The effect is limited to small raw balances but contradicts continuous accrual and can delay otherwise productive presses. Preserve fractional accrual across checkpoints.

**L3 — Public oracle consultation accepts windows below its stated floor. Known open.**

A mature oracle accepts `window = 1`; zero reaches division by zero. Stored-history age is checked, but the effective requested window is not. The shipped button uses the fixed 600-second wrapper. [Consultation](src/TorchOracle.sol:106), [wrapper](src/TorchOracle.sol:125).
