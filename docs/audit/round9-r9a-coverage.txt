**MEDIUM**

**M1 — Successful short transfers still retire full entitlements.**

The claim’s accept set includes a successful transfer that delivers less than the entitlement being retired.

After an upgradeable quote introduces a 1% outbound tax, any caller can trigger payment of a creator’s nominal 100-token claim. The recipient receives 99, while `creatorPaid` increases by 100 and all 100 vault claims are burned. Referrer claims have the same gap. There is no recipient-delivery measurement. [Creator debit](src/TorchHook.sol:615), [referrer debit](src/TorchHook.sol:626), [payout](src/TorchHook.sol:589).

The explicit promise to protect against quotes becoming fee-on-transfer remains. Enforce exact delivery or withdraw that guarantee. [Promise](docs/plan.md:226).

**M2 — A stranger can substitute a pending launch’s economics at its predicted address.**

Factory creation accepts unauthenticated competing intent for the same address commitment.

An attacker copies a pending transaction’s salt, name, symbol and supply, substitutes their own creator address, and deploys first. The intended transaction then fails; its predicted coin address contains the attacker’s fee and allocation recipient. Other economic fields can also be substituted. Single-sided creation requires no contributed quote. [Creation](src/TorchFactory.sol:120), [address commitment](src/TorchFactory.sol:182).

Bind deployment identity to authenticated creation intent and its configuration. This remains explicitly acknowledged as open in the [census](docs/site-census.md:260).

**M3 — Execution still accepts losses beyond the advertised TWAP-relative band.**

For a mature self-buyback pool, an ordering adversary may move spot adversely by 300 ticks. The buyback then permits another 3% movement in **square-root spot price**. With sufficient allowance and constant liquidity, a purchase reaching the upper limit receives approximately:

`1 / (1.03 × 1.0001^300) = 0.94218`

of TWAP-priced output before fees: a **5.78% shortfall**. [Spot admission](src/TorchHook.sol:887), [execution limit](src/TorchHook.sol:509).

Three converter hops can compound allowed adverse displacements with the route tolerance to approximately `0.97 / 1.0001^900 = 0.88652` of corresponding TWAP-priced output before fees. [Hop admission](src/TorchConverter.sol:226), [route floor](src/TorchConverter.sol:184).

These checks do not establish the documented 3% maximum move from the time-weighted price. Specify and test the aggregate bound, or narrow the claim. [Claim](src/TorchConstants.sol:140), [R8](docs/plan.md:427).

**M5 — Deployment and launch-sizing instructions still cause honest failures.**

The deployment recipe supplies two converter constructor arguments; the implementation requires three, including the immutable hook. Following the recipe literally cannot deploy it. [Recipe](README.md:135), [constructor](src/TorchConverter.sol:112).

[Discovery](tools/discover_quotes.py:219), [default](tools/discover_quotes.py:245), [execution refusal](src/TorchConverter.sol:221).

A counterfeit token with the expected symbol and funded liquidity also satisfies discovery’s checks. These properties cannot authenticate its issuer, contrary to the README’s direction to confirm against this output. [Verification](tools/discover_quotes.py:189), [guidance](README.md:108).

**M8 — Critical witnesses remain non-distinguishing; the known mutation fixture is constructible.**

The new small-bucket test makes 200-unit exact-input buys. Each produces a fee of `floor(200 / 101) = 1`, but its 20% self-burn share is zero. Consequently, `_bank` does **not** checkpoint allowance during the loop. Both scaled and unscaled implementations pass. [Test](test/TorchBuyback.t.sol:712), [fee](src/TorchHook.sol:285), [credit/checkpoint](src/TorchHook.sol:963).

**The fixture is constructible using the existing setup:**

- Keep the initial 6,000,000-unit buy: it banks 11,881 self-burn units.
- Make each subsequent buy **505 units**, once per second for 400 seconds.
- Each buy produces a five-unit fee and **one self-burn unit**, forcing a checkpoint.
- Pending ends at **12,281**, remaining below the threshold where whole-unit per-second accrual becomes nonzero.
- The source-derived model gives **201 allowance units with scaling and zero with `ALLOWANCE_SCALE = 1`**.

Assert the per-iteration bucket increase and checkpoint timestamp as well as final allowance. This construction was checked arithmetically, not executed in an EVM here.

Other surviving evidence defects include:

- The hourly witness still uses **60-second steps**, catches failed presses, and omits intervening banking and threshold crossings. The advertised one-second schedule is not its executed schedule. [Witness](test/TorchBuyback.t.sol:588), [invocation](test/TorchBuyback.t.sol:620).
- The shallow-pool witness passes after one successful reduction followed by permanent refusal. [Witness](test/TorchBuyback.t.sol:569).
- The young-pool witness accepts any revert; empty allowance already satisfies it before oracle validation. [Witness](test/TorchBuyback.t.sol:365).
- Four-branch equality compares buys only with 0.1% tolerance; the ordering witness executes no swap. [Comparison](test/TorchFeeTake.t.sol:188), [ordering](test/TorchFeeTake.t.sol:249).
- The real-token freeze witness permits freezing to fail; the isolation fixture disconnects epoch recording and conversion. [Freeze](test/TorchEndToEnd.t.sol:320), [wiring](test/TorchIsolation.t.sol:63).

These are evidence defects, not proof that every corresponding implementation is defective.

**Remaining acceptance/refusal conclusions**

The four fee branches select the quote leg consistently; fee rounding favours the trader by fractional raw units, and split remainders go to the creator. Opening caps count positive coin receipts during the configured blocks and admit sales. They key on `tx.origin`; changing intermediary contracts does not change that origin, so the documented “contract-per-swap” explanation remains inaccurate. [Branches/caps](src/TorchHook.sol:328), [split](src/TorchHook.sol:927), [explanation](docs/plan.md:277).

Factory creation, vesting allocations, venue callbacks, oracle writes and epoch recording retain their respective authorization checks. Vesting pays only its fixed recipient and retires entitlement before transfer. No additional mint, principal-withdrawal, economic setter, owner or upgrade path was found in the shipped source.

For the refusal ledger, R1–R5 retain their intended core predicates; R6–R7 are withdrawn; R8 retains M3; the shipped button’s R9 fails closed. Authenticated accrual supplies R10’s deposit authority, and vesting enforces vested entitlement for R11. R12–R16 belong to the accepted cut. R17 rejects quote-requiring seeds; R18 atomically rejects specified-quote partial fills. [Ledger](docs/plan.md:420).

The conversion withdrawal remains atomic at every hop: a later refusal unwinds earlier swaps and claim retirement, while direct quote claiming remains available under ordinary token semantics. I found no additional entitlement stranded behind that boundary. Self-buyback accepts partial fills and debits realised spend; zero output reverts all intermediate state. No reachable reentrancy construction changing this pool’s pending between allowance calculation and debit was established. [Per-hop refusal](src/TorchConverter.sol:262), [buyback settlement](src/TorchHook.sol:752).

No platform two-press transition survives in this checkout. Its absence, the unspent platform share and the absence of v1 pot payouts are accepted scope boundaries.
