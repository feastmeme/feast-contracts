**HIGH**

**H1 — The new route bound still accepts the attacker-owned route from round 2.**

The converter derives expected output from the supplied pools’ current square-root prices, then compares execution against that expectation. Nothing independently bounds the quote-to-native exchange value. [src/TorchConverter.sol:150](src/TorchConverter.sol:150), [src/TorchConverter.sol:184](src/TorchConverter.sol:184), [src/TorchConverter.sol:268](src/TorchConverter.sol:268)

Construction: an attacker creates ordinary ERC-20 X and genuine Q/X and X/WBNB pools, with mature observations and constant prices of approximately one X per Q and `0.000001` WBNB per X. Supply X-side liquidity to receive Q and sufficient WBNB liquidity for the second hop. Both spot/TWAP deviations can be zero.

For 100 Q, with 500-pip fees at both hops, the converter expects approximately `0.000099900025` WBNB. An execution returning `0.000099800125` passes comfortably. The attacker withdraws the valuable Q received by their first LP position; the protocol receives negligible native value.

The platform button accepts this caller-selected route. Its final token-identity check does not repair the earlier loss. [src/TorchHook.sol:920](src/TorchHook.sol:920)

The new test instead makes a mock underpay its own quoted price. That does not exercise malicious but internally consistent prices. [test/TorchConverterBounds.t.sol:146](test/TorchConverterBounds.t.sol:146)

**H2 — Successful partial fills still strand three kinds of entitlement.**

The accept set remains too wide at three settlement boundaries:

1. **Claimant conversion:** a creator claims 100 Q; the first hop consumes 10 and returns positive native output. The converter refunds 90 Q to the hook, but `_claimTo` has retired all 100 and `claimAsBnb` neither pays nor recredits the refund. [src/TorchHook.sol:734](src/TorchHook.sol:734), [src/TorchHook.sol:753](src/TorchHook.sol:753), [src/TorchConverter.sol:190](src/TorchConverter.sol:190)
2. **Intermediate partial fill:** a later hop exhausts a narrow liquidity range after consuming, for example, 98% of its offered intermediate token. With small execution impact before exhaustion, total output can remain inside the 3% route bound. The remaining intermediate token stays in the converter: only the original input is refunded. [src/TorchConverter.sol:160](src/TorchConverter.sol:160), [src/TorchConverter.sol:171](src/TorchConverter.sol:171)
3. **Final platform purchase:** conversion produces WBNB, but the Infinity purchase reaches its price limit before consuming it all. The callback returns only purchased coins. Positive output completes the press, while remaining WBNB stays in the hook without an entitlement or recovery path. [src/TorchHook.sol:639](src/TorchHook.sol:639), [src/TorchHook.sol:938](src/TorchHook.sol:938)

Reconcile every asset with its beneficiary, or atomically refuse unsupported partial fills.

[src/TorchConverter.sol:133](src/TorchConverter.sol:133), [src/TorchConverter.sol:138](src/TorchConverter.sol:138), [src/TorchConverter.sol:143](src/TorchConverter.sol:143)

WBNB-denominated `claimAsBnb` has the same over-refusal. [src/TorchHook.sol:740](src/TorchHook.sol:740), [src/TorchHook.sol:920](src/TorchHook.sol:920)

**H4 — Dust-positive presses can still suppress productive buybacks indefinitely.**

Both buttons require positive input and positive purchased output, but no meaningful progress before consuming the full hourly interval. [src/TorchHook.sol:799](src/TorchHook.sol:799), [src/TorchHook.sol:829](src/TorchHook.sol:829), [src/TorchHook.sol:943](src/TorchHook.sol:943)

**H5 — Reentrancy during conversion bypasses the platform interval and overstates remaining entitlement. Fresh.**

`platformBuyBack` caches `pending`, checks the old cooldown, and calls external conversion before updating either. After conversion it assigns `pending - quoteSpent`, using the cached value. [src/TorchHook.sol:892](src/TorchHook.sol:892), [src/TorchHook.sol:905](src/TorchHook.sol:905), [src/TorchHook.sol:920](src/TorchHook.sol:920), [src/TorchHook.sol:927](src/TorchHook.sol:927)

Concrete construction:

1. A closed source epoch has 1,000 Q pending. Start a permitted 200 Q press through Q → X → WBNB.
2. X is an attacker-controlled ERC-20. When the converter pays X into the second V3 pool, X re-enters `platformBuyBack` for the same source and epoch, using a separate mature route.
3. The inner call still sees 1,000 pending and the old cooldown. It spends another 200 Q, writes pending 800, increments `platformUsed` by 200, and completes.
4. The outer call resumes and also writes pending 800, while incrementing `platformUsed` by another 200.

Actual remaining entitlement is 600; the epoch mapping advertises 800. Both presses succeeded in one interval. Repetition can spend claims backing creator/referrer balances or other pools sharing Q.

This callback occurs during the converter’s token payment, **after** its active-pool caller check. The inner conversion clearing `_activePool` does not stop the already-admitted outer callback. [src/TorchConverter.sol:307](src/TorchConverter.sol:307)

The Infinity vault is unlocked during conversion. This construction does not rely on callbacks during hook-originated Infinity swaps. Reserve state before external calls or guard the entire operation against reentrancy; zero-output failure can still roll that reservation back.

**H6 — Per-funding index truncation can make an entire funded pot unclaimable. Fresh.**

Every credit independently floors `amount * 1e27 / denominator`, discarding the remainder. Entitlements depend on the accumulated index, rather than cumulative pot funding. [src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:78](src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:78), [src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:162](src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:162), [src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:170](src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:170)

Construction: a closed epoch has one payer and denominator `10^30`. Platform presses credit 100 and then 900 raw platform units. Both index increments are zero. The pot contains 1,000 tokens and the sole payer can claim zero. One combined credit of 1,000 would instead make all 1,000 claimable.

This can also strand funding after an earlier successful claim. The factory admits arbitrary ERC-20 quote denominations and platform supply sizes; no invariant relates the denominator to index precision. [src/TorchFactory.sol:113](src/TorchFactory.sol:113), [src/TorchFactory.sol:120](src/TorchFactory.sol:120)

The sequence fuzz restricts fee amounts to at most `1e24` and funding to at least `1e18`, excluding this construction. [test/TorchEpochRewards.t.sol:137](test/TorchEpochRewards.t.sol:137), [test/TorchEpochRewards.t.sol:145](test/TorchEpochRewards.t.sol:145)

Because closed denominators are fixed, cumulative-pot pro-rata accounting with cumulative amounts claimed can avoid this per-funding loss.

Another caller can copy name, symbol, supply and salt from a pending launch, substitute creator, quote, shares or allocation, and deploy first at the same predicted address. **M3 — Unsolicited converter balances can block otherwise valid conversions. Fresh.**

`unspent` is the converter’s entire input-token balance, including pre-existing tokens. The subtraction from this call’s `amountIn` can underflow. [src/TorchConverter.sol:171](src/TorchConverter.sol:171)

Construction: send 101 Q directly to the converter before a fully filled 100 Q conversion. The remaining balance is 101; `100 - 101` reverts. A closed platform bucket capped at 100 Q per press cannot clear that balance through any of its permitted presses. A sufficiently larger unrelated conversion may clear it, but that is not an assured recovery path.

Measure balances attributable to the current operation.

**M4 — The advertised 3% TWAP bound is still not enforced across execution sites.**

A mature pool at an allowed adverse spot, followed by further adverse execution, therefore passes outside the claimed TWAP band.
