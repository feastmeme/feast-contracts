**HIGH**

**H1 — The new allowance permits substantially more than 20% to leave within an hour. Fresh mechanism.**

The buyback’s accept set exceeds B4’s explicit promise that hourly spending remains unchanged. A full allowance can be spent immediately, followed by its refill during the same hour. Capping the *available allowance* does not cap *cumulative spending*. [plan.md:748](docs/plan.md:748), [TorchHook.sol:797](src/TorchHook.sol:797)

Concrete construction: an ordinary caller uses a mature, sufficiently deep pool with pending self-burn funds of `1,000e18`, full allowance, and spot near TWAP. No further fees accrue.

| Time | Accepted realised spend | Pending afterwards |
|---|---:|---:|
| `t` | `200e18` | `800e18` |
| `t + 1,800 seconds` | `80e18` | `720e18` |

That spends **28% in 30 minutes**. Alternatively, waiting 3,599 seconds before the second press permits approximately **35.9956% within one hour**. Both presses can fill completely with negligible price movement in a deep pool. The allowance calculation and debit admit this directly. [TorchHook.sol:745](src/TorchHook.sol:745), [TorchHook.sol:765](src/TorchHook.sol:765), [TorchHook.sol:806](src/TorchHook.sol:806)

There is a second rate-accounting defect: `_bank` increases pending without checkpointing allowance accrual. The next read applies the **new** cap to the **entire** elapsed interval. For example, after spending the initial allowance, leave pending at 800 tokens for 3,599 seconds, then bank another 400,000 immediately before pressing. The calculated allowance is approximately 80,137.73 tokens; only approximately 159.96 accrued against the previously existing bucket. Newly credited funds receive almost an hour of retroactive allowance. [TorchHook.sol:809](src/TorchHook.sol:809), [TorchHook.sol:914](src/TorchHook.sol:914)

This increases the protocol funds exposed to public execution and sandwiches beyond the ratified pacing bound. Enforce an explicit cumulative hourly spending invariant and checkpoint accrual whenever its underlying bucket changes. Tests must sum realised spending across sequences, including fee accrual between presses.

**MEDIUM**

**M1 — The allowance still strands buckets whose capped input cannot buy one raw coin unit. Known open.**

The fixed quote-unit exemption does not account for coin-output granularity. Above 10,000 raw quote units, waiting never raises allowance above 20% of pending, and zero output reverts. [TorchConstants.sol:117](src/TorchConstants.sol:117), [TorchHook.sol:797](src/TorchHook.sol:797), [TorchHook.sol:757](src/TorchHook.sol:757)

An admitted construction uses a six-decimal quote, coin as currency0, supply 1,000 raw coin units, opening tick 138200, and liquidity approximately 1,001,901. The position consumes the seed allocation. After the opening cap expires, buying 300 raw coins exact-output and selling them back accrues approximately 1.72 million raw quote units to self-burn. Let the oracle mature at the resulting price. The factory admits these parameters. [TorchFactory.sol:105](src/TorchFactory.sol:105), [TorchFactory.sol:135](src/TorchFactory.sol:135)

The approximately 344,000-unit allowance buys **zero** raw coins; spending the whole bucket would buy **one**, within the price bound. Collecting the small outstanding LP income does not close that gap. With trading stopped, every allowed press fails indefinitely. The six-decimal assumption does not constrain coin granularity.

**M2 — CREATE2 permits substitution of a pending launch’s economics. Known open.**

Another caller can copy salt, name, symbol and supply, substitute creator, quote, shares, allocation and opening configuration, and deploy first. Those substituted fields are absent from the address commitment. Single-sided creation requires no contributed quote. The intended transaction then fails while its predicted address contains different economics. [TorchFactory.sol:120](src/TorchFactory.sol:120), [TorchFactory.sol:182](src/TorchFactory.sol:182)

Bind deployment identity to authenticated creation intent and economic configuration.

**M3 — Claims retire nominal entitlement without enforcing delivery. Known open.**

If an upgradeable quote begins taxing outbound transfers, anyone can trigger a creator claim of 100 that delivers 99 while permanently retiring all 100. Referrer claims have the same acceptance gap. The payout burns the nominal vault claim and requests a transfer without checking recipient delivery. [TorchHook.sol:610](src/TorchHook.sol:610), [TorchHook.sol:621](src/TorchHook.sol:621), [TorchHook.sol:589](src/TorchHook.sol:589)

This contradicts the explicit transfer-delta protection promised for quote upgrades. [plan.md:226](docs/plan.md:226)

**M4 — Execution accepts losses beyond the advertised TWAP-relative band. Known open.**

Self-buyback permits a 300-tick adverse spot displacement, then applies its limit to **square-root spot price**. A constant-liquidity currency0 purchase reaching the `1.03 × sqrtPrice` limit can deliver approximately:

`1 / (1.03 × 1.0001^300) = 0.94218`

of TWAP-priced output before fees—a 5.78% shortfall. [TorchHook.sol:509](src/TorchHook.sol:509), [TorchHook.sol:851](src/TorchHook.sol:851)

Three converter hops can compound adverse spot displacements with the additional route allowance to approximately `0.97 / 1.0001^900 = 0.88652` of TWAP-priced output. The checks do not establish R8’s aggregate economic bound. [TorchConverter.sol:184](src/TorchConverter.sol:184), [TorchConverter.sol:226](src/TorchConverter.sol:226), [plan.md:427](docs/plan.md:427)
