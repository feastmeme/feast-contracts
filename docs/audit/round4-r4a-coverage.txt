**HIGH**

**H1 — Permissionless conversion still accepts economically destructive attacker-owned routes.**

`convertPlatformQuote` lets any caller select the route. The converter derives expected output from those selected pools’ spot prices, checks each against its own history, and accepts output within 3% of that expectation. There is no independent quote/native valuation. [src/TorchHook.sol:867](src/TorchHook.sol:867), [src/TorchConverter.sol:150](src/TorchConverter.sol:150), [src/TorchConverter.sol:264](src/TorchConverter.sol:264)

Construction: an attacker supplies genuine, mature Q/X and X/WBNB pools, using an ordinary attacker-issued X. Their prices exchange valuable Q for X and X for negligible WBNB. Supply enough directional liquidity to fill the entire request, with little price impact. Both spot/TWAP deviations can be zero, and execution can closely match the advertised prices.

The attacker presses an allowed amount from Q’s platform bucket, receives Q through their liquidity, and leaves negligible native value settled for the protocol. Full consumption satisfies the new refusal. Splitting the purchase into another transaction does not repair the completed loss.

**H2 — The withdrawal does not cover later route hops or the final platform purchase.**

Two successful partial-fill paths still retire entitlement without disposing of its remainder:

1. **Intermediate token:** the first Q→X hop fills completely. The X→WBNB hop exhausts a narrow liquidity range after consuming approximately 98% of X. With small price impact, its output remains above the route’s 97% floor. The final fill check inspects only Q, whose balance is zero. The call succeeds with approximately 2% of X stranded in the converter. `_hop` returns output without checking its input delta. [src/TorchConverter.sol:161](src/TorchConverter.sol:161), [src/TorchConverter.sol:174](src/TorchConverter.sol:174), [src/TorchConverter.sol:241](src/TorchConverter.sol:241)
2. **Final platform purchase:** start with 100 WBNB recorded as settled native; request an allowed 20. A shallow platform pool reaches the imposed price limit after consuming 1 WBNB and returns positive platform tokens. The ledger becomes 80, while actual native holdings become 99. The remaining 19 has no entitlement. The callback settles actual debt but returns only purchased output; the caller checks only that output is nonzero. [src/TorchHook.sol:909](src/TorchHook.sol:909), [src/TorchHook.sol:627](src/TorchHook.sol:627), [src/TorchHook.sol:913](src/TorchHook.sol:913)

These violate the declared withdrawal boundary. Enforcing full consumption at these settlement sites would implement that boundary without restoring a refund mechanism.

**H3 — Wrapped-native platform buckets still have no spending path.**

For WBNB input:

- An empty route fails `EmptyRoute`.
- Every nonempty route must end in WBNB.
- Any occurrence of the input token in the route fails `RouteRevisitsAToken`.

These conditions are mutually exclusive. Both hook callers still invoke the converter unconditionally. [src/TorchConverter.sol:134](src/TorchConverter.sol:134), [src/TorchConverter.sol:139](src/TorchConverter.sol:139), [src/TorchConverter.sol:144](src/TorchConverter.sol:144), [src/TorchHook.sol:728](src/TorchHook.sol:728), [src/TorchHook.sol:867](src/TorchHook.sol:867)

Ordinary trading in the required platform-coin/WBNB pool therefore accumulates a platform allocation that cannot reach `platformNative`. Smaller requests and deeper pools cannot resolve the contradictory route requirements. WBNB-denominated `claimAsBnb` is also refused, although direct claims remain available.

**H4 — Positive dust can still suppress productive buybacks indefinitely.**

Both purchase buttons accept arbitrarily small positive output and consume the full hourly interval. [src/TorchHook.sol:817](src/TorchHook.sol:817), [src/TorchHook.sol:908](src/TorchHook.sol:908), [src/TorchHook.sol:913](src/TorchHook.sol:913)

Construction: with mature pools and substantial pending balances, front-run a productive press using an input that purchases only a few raw coin units. The tip may round to zero. The press succeeds, and the productive transaction fails for an hour. Repeat at each expiry.

The zero-output refusal changes the minimum attack amount; it does not establish meaningful progress before occupying the interval.

**H5 — A one-unit donation now permanently disables conversions starting with that token. Worsened by the withdrawal.**

The fill check subtracts the converter’s **entire** input-token balance from this call’s input, including unsolicited balances. [src/TorchConverter.sol:174](src/TorchConverter.sol:174)

Construction:

1. Transfer one raw unit of ordinary ERC-20 Q directly to the converter.
2. An honest conversion transfers N additional Q and fully spends N.
3. One Q remains; computed consumption is `N − 1`, so `RouteWouldNotFill` reverts.

The donation predates the reverted call and remains. Every positive request size fails. A larger request no longer clears the balance because the refund/sweep was deleted. Using Q as an intermediate token also spends only that hop’s offered amount and leaves its pre-existing balance intact. [src/TorchConverter.sol:157](src/TorchConverter.sol:157), [src/TorchConverter.sol:162](src/TorchConverter.sol:162)

This permanently bricks platform conversion for **every pool sharing Q**, including future accrual. Direct beneficiary claims and self-buyback remain available, but neither can dispose of the platform bucket. Measure consumption attributable to the operation, or use the authenticated swap input delta.

**H6 — A whole accrued reward pot can still become permanently unclaimable.**

Closing an epoch floors `accrued × 1e27 / total`, clears its accrual, and marks it finalized. Claims subsequently depend on that index. [src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:203](src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:203), [src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:217](src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:217), [src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:233](src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:233)

Construction: one payer pays `10^30` raw quote units during E. Purchases during E accrue 999 raw platform units. At closure:

`floor(999 × 10^27 / 10^30) = 0`

All 999 funded units remain held, while the sole payer’s entitlement is zero forever. The factory places no denomination relationship between quote fees and platform supply that makes this loss necessarily economically negligible. [src/TorchFactory.sol:113](src/TorchFactory.sol:113), [src/TorchFactory.sol:120](src/TorchFactory.sol:120)

The production-path failure requires only ordinary `creditPot` and closure. Although `creditPotClosed` also retains per-credit truncation, the shipped hook has no call to that entry point; it is not needed for this construction.

**M1 — Splitting the platform press removed pacing from quote conversion. Fresh.**

`convertPlatformQuote` has a proportional size ceiling and reentrancy guard, but no interval. The interval now applies only to spending already-converted native. [src/TorchHook.sol:852](src/TorchHook.sol:852), [src/TorchHook.sol:900](src/TorchHook.sol:900)

On sufficiently deep, mature routes, a caller can make ten sequential conversions in one transaction, each spending 20% of the remaining bucket. Together they convert approximately **89.26%** of its initial balance. `nonReentrant` permits sequential calls.

**M2 — Delayed finalization lets callers choose which later epoch receives an empty epoch’s pot. Fresh.**

The interface promises rollover into the next epoch. Implementation instead selects `currentEpoch()` when somebody eventually calls `finalise`. [docs/interface.md:214](docs/interface.md:214), [src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:210](src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:210)

Construction: E receives a pot but has no Q fee payers. Alice pays Q fees in E+1. Nobody finalizes E yet. In E+2, Bob pays a small fee and calls `finalise(E, Q)`. The entire pot moves to E+2, skipping Alice’s closed, nonempty epoch. Bob can receive it if he remains that epoch’s sole payer.

Thus eligibility depends on maintenance-call timing. The regression covers only immediate next-epoch finalization. [test/TorchEpochRewards.t.sol:194](test/TorchEpochRewards.t.sol:194)
