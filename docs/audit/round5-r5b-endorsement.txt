**HIGH**

**H1 — Permissionless conversion still accepts economically destructive routes.**

The presser selects the pools supplying both execution prices and the expected-output calculation. Each pool is compared only with its own history; no independent quote/WBNB valuation constrains their product. [Route valuation](src/TorchConverter.sol:149), [hop validation](src/TorchConverter.sol:211).

An attacker can establish mature Q/X and X/WBNB pools in which valuable Q buys negligible WBNB. With sufficient liquidity relative to the press, both hops consume their entire inputs, follow their advertised prices and satisfy the output floor. The attacker’s liquidity receives Q; the platform receives negligible value. An attacker-controlled intermediate token can restrict competing arbitrage while permitting the converter.

The hook permits any caller to select this route. Restoring the hourly interval limits its frequency but does not make a permitted execution safe. [Platform conversion](src/TorchHook.sol:860).

The regression makes a mock underpay its advertised price; it does not test faithful execution at an economically destructive advertised price. [Current regression](test/TorchConverterBounds.t.sol:146).

Protocol spending needs a valuation constraint independent of the presser’s selected pools.

**H2 — The required WBNB platform bucket has no spending path.**

For WBNB input, the converter’s route requirements are contradictory:

- An empty route is refused.
- Every accepted route ends in WBNB.
- No output token may equal the input token.

Consequently, **every route for WBNB input is refused**, regardless of depth or request size. [Route checks](src/TorchConverter.sol:133).

The platform coin’s required WBNB pool accrues the ordinary 20% platform allocation, but `convertPlatformQuote` invokes this converter unconditionally. `platformBuyBack` spends only previously credited `platformNative`; it cannot consume the stranded vault claims. [Conversion](src/TorchHook.sol:892), [settled-native requirement](src/TorchHook.sol:927).

An already-wrapped-native settlement path is required. The same route contradiction also prevents WBNB-denominated `claimAsBnb`, although direct claims remain available.

**H3 — Positive dust can occupy all three buttons’ hourly intervals.**

Self-buyback and platform purchasing require only positive coin output. A caller can front-run productive spending with the smallest purchase producing a few raw units, consume the hour, and repeat at expiry. [Self-buyback](src/TorchHook.sol:832), [platform purchase](src/TorchHook.sol:941).

**The restored conversion clock adds another site to this class.** A tiny, fully filled conversion producing positive WBNB now occupies the source pool’s entire conversion interval. Its tip may round to zero, and there is no minimum progress requirement relative to the available bucket. [Conversion clock and output check](src/TorchHook.sol:878).

The existing tests cover zero output and ordinary repeated presses, not positive dust suppressing meaningful spending. The census acknowledges the remaining defect. [Known-open record](docs/site-census.md:333).

Require meaningful progress before consuming an interval at every button site.

**H4 — Finalisation can still strand an entire funded reward pot.**

The contract computes:

`index = floor(accrued × 10^27 / totalFees)`

and then:

`entitlement = floor(payerFees × index / 10^27)`.

With one payer, `totalFees = 10^30` and funding of 999 raw platform units, the index and every claim are zero. All 999 units remain held permanently. The denominator is positive, so empty-epoch rollover does not apply. [Finalisation](src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:209), [entitlement](src/TorchEpochRewards.sol:231).

No launch restriction excludes this denomination relationship. Raw units are not guaranteed economically insignificant across arbitrary supplies.

The fuzz test excludes this regime by bounding individual fees at `10^24` and each funding at no less than `10^18`. [Fuzz bounds](test/TorchEpochRewards.t.sol:145).

Use cumulative pro-rata arithmetic that preserves the pot through division, or otherwise retain the discarded entitlement. These funds are allocated rewards, not B2 stray balances.

**M3 — The advertised 3% TWAP bound remains broader than execution protection.**

Three adverse 300-tick spot/TWAP displacements, followed by the route’s permitted 3% shortfall against **spot-derived** expected output, permit approximately:

`0.97 / 1.0001^900 = 0.8865`

of corresponding TWAP-priced output, before pool fees. [Spot comparison](src/TorchConverter.sol:226), [route floor](src/TorchConverter.sol:179).

The hook’s purchase limits multiply square-root price by 0.97 or 1.03, permitting terminal price ratios of 0.9409 or 1.0609 before the initial spot displacement. [Purchase limit](src/TorchHook.sol:523).

Define the intended economic bound accurately and test against it. Full consumption establishes quantity spent, not an advertised TWAP-relative execution value.

**Completeness**

The mandate is **partly solved. I would not endorse this tree for internal v1.**

| Fee share | Endpoint |
|---|---|
| Self-burn | Normally buys and genuinely burns the coin, less the caller tip. Unspent quote remains represented by vault claims. Positive-dust suppression remains. |
| Referrer | Healthy-token direct claims reach the recorded referrer. Optional conversion delivers WBNB. Taxed delivery is unchecked. |
| Creator | Direct claims and fixed-recipient vesting work. Burn-address creator fees redirect to self-buyback. The same conversion and delivery limitations apply. |
| Platform | Full-fill refusal now preserves custody atomically, but destructive routing, unspendable WBNB buckets, dust suppression and reward truncation still prevent assured disposition. |

LP collection exists and preserves principal. Its quote-side platform share redirects to self-buyback; coin-side fees go to the designated burn address. [LP settlement](src/TorchHook.sol:562), [platform-share redirection](src/TorchHook.sol:1080).

I found no owner, upgrade, pause or negative-liquidity path in the shipped modules. The governor restricts proposals to one target and selector; the callee enforces the band and next-epoch activation. I found no remaining inconsistent entitlement between the two platform presses under ordinary token semantics: conversion credits settled WBNB, and purchasing either spends its exact debit or reverts.

Endorsement requires fixing the four remaining HIGH classes, making deployment executable, reconciling the normative contract, and replacing the identified adjacent assertions with effective witnesses.
