**L1 — The revised `claim` notice incorrectly promises at most one settlement.**

The new notice says `claim` pays “up to one settlement,” but its body calls `claimCreator` and then `claimReferrer`. Each independently opens a bounded payout lock. An account entitled to both components can therefore receive more than `MAX_SETTLEMENT` in one transaction. [Notice and wrapper](src/TorchHook.sol:654)

For creator entitlement `C`, referral entitlement `R` and ceiling `M`, direct claiming pays:

`min(C, M) + min(R, M)`

The single shared budget applies to `_claimTo`, used by `claimAsBnb`. [Shared budget](src/TorchHook.sol:709)

The new partial-referral fixture supplies a concrete reachable construction: both components fit individually, but their sum exceeds `M`. An integer reconstruction of its 215 referred buys gives approximately `1.7029703e38` combined entitlement against `M ≈ 1.7014118e38`. Direct `claim` pays both components through separate settlements; conversion pays only `M`.

This does **not** violate the venue’s per-settlement ceiling or lose funds. Correct the notice to say: “Pays each creator/referrer component up to one settlement; a combined claim may make two settlements.” No contract behavior change is needed.
